# Content Policy and Reporting

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective Date:** October 15, 2026 · Verisphere Corp.

<!-- patch_report_path -->

This page says what may not be posted, how to report something, and what happens next. It restates §8–9 of the [Terms of Service](viewer.html?file=tos.md) in plain words; the Terms govern if they differ.

## What may not be posted

Claims are statements of fact that others stake on. The Service will not relay, and will hide from display, content that:

- contains hate speech, slurs, or calls for violence against any group;
- is sexually explicit, or sexualizes minors in any way;
- threatens violence against a person or a group;
- gives instructions for making weapons, explosives or dangerous substances;
- exposes someone's private information — home address, phone number, documents, or anything else that identifies and locates a private person (doxxing).

Claims that are merely wrong, contested or unpopular are **not** violations. Being wrong is what the stake is for: challenge it.

## How moderation works

Two automated checks run: one before the relay submits a claim, one when a claim is displayed. They are automated and can err in both directions. A human reviews reports.

## Reporting a claim

Every claim has a **Report** link. It opens an e-mail to **abuse@verisphere.co** with the claim number and text filled in; add why you are reporting it. We read every report. Please do not report claims because you disagree with them — stake against them instead.

## What happens after a report

1. We look at the claim against the list above, normally within two business days.
2. If it violates the policy, we **hide its text** from the app. The claim itself cannot be removed — it is on the Avalanche blockchain, which nobody controls — and its stakes, score and links stay visible and keep settling. The text shows as *"[Content hidden — removed by Verisphere]"* wherever it appeared.
3. If it does not, nothing changes, and we reply only if you asked a question.
4. Hiding is logged (who, when, why) and can be reversed. If your claim was hidden and you think that was wrong, write to abuse@verisphere.co with the claim number.

Hiding affects only what the Verisphere app shows. Anyone reading the blockchain directly, or using another interface to the protocol, still sees the text; we cannot change that.

## Legal requests

Requests from authorities or rights-holders go to **info@verisphere.co**, with the claim number and the basis for the request.
